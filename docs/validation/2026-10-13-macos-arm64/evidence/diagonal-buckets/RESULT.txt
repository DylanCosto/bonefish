The change selects in-band predecessors from neighboring diagonal buckets
on dense repetitive reads, then uses the existing NEON score kernel.
Predecessor order, ties, skip/visited behavior, no-skip mode, ordinary-read
fallback, and backtracking are preserved. An additional differential test
caught a direct-port error at the signed 32-bit coordinate boundary. The
final implementation uses the original contiguous scan for such positions.

Validation passed: 125 core tests, 5 integration tests, and 6 doctests
(136 total); 3 existing benchmark tests remained ignored. Five new NEON
tests compare DP scores, predecessor choices, and chain output with the
original optimized scan, plus scalar comparisons where skip semantics match.
They cover dense tandem repeats, 24 randomized cases, target/strand changes,
negative diagonals, bucket/window thresholds, no-skip mode, empty/no-chain
reads, scratch reuse, and signed-coordinate crossings. Existing golden SAM/
PAF variants, BAM/FASTQ/BGZF input, raw indexes, and sorted BAM checks passed.

Scope: one nominal 1x HG002 ONT ultra-long dataset, 3,088,602,282 bases,
equal-base prefixes of four Dorado 0.4.0 SUP 4.1.0 source parts, aligned to
the full GRCh38 no-alt analysis set. This sample is not uniformly random.
Two runs per final build establish an observed gain here, not a general
speed guarantee. Alignment choices/MAPQ are preserved, not independently
requalified. Linux/x86 execution was not tested; the production change is
restricted to aarch64. Earlier candidate-1/2 receipts describe the exploratory
port before the coordinate guard and are excluded from the final averages.
